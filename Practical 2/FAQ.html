Frequently Asked Questions(FAQ)

How can I check my timetable?

Students can check their latest class timetable through the StudentHub Portal. The timetable includes subjects, classrooms, faculty names, and lecture timings.

Where can I find my exam schedule?

The exam schedule is available in the Examination section of StudentHub. Students can check the date, time, subject, and examination venue.

How can I check my attendance?

Students can view their subject-wise attendance by logging into their StudentHub account. The attendance section shows the percentage of attendance for each subject.

Where can I download study materials?

Study materials such as notes, presentations, assignments, and previous question papers can be downloaded from the Study Materials section of StudentHub.

What scholarships are available for students?

Students can find information about available scholarships, eligibility criteria, required documents, and application deadlines in the Scholarship section of StudentHub.
